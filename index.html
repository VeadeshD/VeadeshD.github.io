About Me

My engineering experience spans the full hardware lifecycle: from writing deterministic SystemVerilog RTL and synthesizing FPGA architectures, to designing custom PCBs in KiCAD and executing physical SMT/THT manufacturing on the factory floor.


Most recently, I architected an open-source Full Authority Digital Engine Control (FADEC) system on a Lattice iCE40 FPGA, utilizing hardware-accelerated SystemVerilog to regulate electric propulsion at nanosecond speeds. Beyond the technical logic, I prioritize cultivating strong, open-minded relationships with my teammates, as I believe the best engineering solutions come from fresh, collaborative perspectives.
